Your book belongs to you.

Privacy Policy for Wo De Me Ka Credit and Loan Tracker, developed by MaraQueens Corp. Written in plain language so that everyone can understand it.

1. Your information stays on your phone

Wo De Me Ka saves every customer name, phone number, credit, payment and note directly on your device.

We do not upload your book to any server. We do not have a copy of it. We cannot read it, sell it, or share it with anyone.

2. No account, no sign-in

The app does not ask you to register, and it does not collect your email, your contacts, or your location. Everything works completely offline.

An internet connection is only used if you choose to open WhatsApp, call somebody, or view an advertisement.

3. Keeping your data safe

  • You can turn on a 4-digit PIN lock in Settings so that only you can open the app.
  • The PIN is checked on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.
  • Save a backup file regularly. If you lose or change your phone and you have no backup, the data cannot be recovered — not even by us.

4. Backup files you create

When you tap “Save Backup File”, the app creates a single file containing your book. That file is saved by you, wherever you choose (phone storage, WhatsApp, email, cloud drive).

Once the file leaves the app, keeping it private and secure becomes your responsibility.

5. Advertising

Wo De Me Ka is free to use and is supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers to show more relevant ads and to limit how often the same ad appears.

We never share your customers' names, phone numbers, credit records or payments with any advertiser. You can hide banner adverts for 24 hours at any time from the Settings screen.

6. Children

The app is built for adults running a business. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app works, we will update this policy inside the app and revise the date below. Continuing to use Wo De Me Ka means you accept the updated policy.
